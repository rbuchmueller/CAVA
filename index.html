Definition and use case

Definition

A PathMatrix is a cohort-flow view over an ordered sequence of numeric variables. Each row partitions one variable into explicit bins. Links aggregate observations that occupy a pair of bins in adjacent rows. The sequence may encode a hypothesis, workflow, or known causal model; the visualization itself does not infer causality.

Example use case

A clinician compares how patients with different hypovolemia scores are distributed across cardiac volume, stroke volume, cardiac output, and blood-pressure bins. Thick links reveal dominant physiological profiles; thin or unexpected links identify subgroups worth investigating. Cohorts can later be compared before and after treatment.
